Closed testing requirements

  • At least 12 testers opted in to a closed test, for 14 days in a row.
  • Internal and open testing do not count. Testers who leave early stop counting.
  • Then you apply for production; the form asks about your testing.
  1. Day 1: 12+ testers opt in and install
  2. Days 1–14: stay opted in
  3. After 14 days: apply for production in Play Console
