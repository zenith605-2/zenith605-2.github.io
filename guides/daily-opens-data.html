Nobody opens your app all 14 days

Measured 31 August 2026 · 21 finished trades

Everyone guesses at this. We can measure it, because the app records daily opens automatically from Android's usage stats rather than asking anyone to report them.

Here are 21 test-for-test trades that ran their full 14 days, and how many of those days the tester actually opened the app:

Lowest4 / 14
Highest13 / 14
Average10.3 / 14
Opened every single day0 people

Not one. Including the person who built the app.

Two things follow from this

Your test is probably fine

Google counts how long a tester stayed opted in, not how often they opened anything. A tester who missed three days has not broken your test — see what Google actually checks. If everyone had to hit 14 of 14, nobody would ever pass.

But 4 of 14 is not testing

10 of 14 is a person who used your app. 4 of 14 is a person who opted in and forgot. Google may not care about the difference. The developer who installed your app in exchange does.

That is the number worth being embarrassed about — not Google's.

Why this is hard to see

Most tools ask testers to prove it by hand: upload a screenshot, fill in a staged feedback form, stay in a locked group. Every one of those adds work, and work is exactly what people stop doing on day five.

Android already keeps a usage log. Reading it costs the tester nothing — they open the app, and the day is recorded. That is the only way we got these numbers at all.
