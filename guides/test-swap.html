How to swap closed tests

A new personal developer account needs 12 testers opted in to a closed test for 14 days before production opens.

1. Open your three links from another account

  • Google Group — private by default; otherwise "Content unavailable". Setup
  • Opt-in link — must show "Become a tester". Closed until a release passes review.
  • Countries — missing country means "not available". Setup

2. Internal testing does not count
