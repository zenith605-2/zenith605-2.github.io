Closed testing 14-day calculator

Enter when each tester opted in. See the first day 12 of them have 14 continuous days. Free, no sign-up, runs in your browser.

How it is calculated

Google's rule for new personal developer accounts is a closed test with at least 12 testers who have been opted in for at least the last 14 days continuously (Google Play Console Help). So the clock runs per tester, from their opt-in, and someone who leaves stops counting. The calculator looks for the first day on which 12 testers each have 14 unbroken days behind them.

  • The 12 do not have to join on the same day. The date is set by the 12th to reach 14 days.
  • If someone leaves, they no longer count — add a replacement and keep the others opted in.
  • Opening the app every day is not part of this rule. See what Google actually checks.

Questions people ask

Is day 14 or day 15 the day I can apply?
Count 14 full days from the opt-in date: someone who opted in on the 1st has 14 days on the 15th. The console can lag by about a day.
What if a tester opts out and joins again?
Their continuous stretch starts again from the new opt-in. Enter the new date as a separate tester.
Does uninstalling the app count as leaving?
Google's rule is written in terms of being opted in. Ask testers to stay opted in until you launch.
