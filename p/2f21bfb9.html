We built a tester board app on Reddit

zenith.studio · 2026-09-17

We made a Reddit app with Devvit (Reddit's developer platform) for the same problem this app is about: getting 12 testers for Google Play closed testing. It lives in our new community, r/AndroidTesterBoard. How it works: you post your closed test on the board. Someone applies, you accept them, they install and send a screenshot, and you confirm it. A swap only counts once both sides confirm, so people can't collect testers and disappear. If you use Reddit, please join and post your app there too. It gives you another place to find swap partners, and every app on the board makes it more useful for the next person. https://www.reddit.com/r/AndroidTesterBoard
