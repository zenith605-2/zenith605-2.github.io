⑥ Setting up a Google Group for your testers

zenith.studio · 2026-09-04

This is the question we get most: "why can't my testers join my group?" There are two ways to let people into a closed test: an email list, or a Google Group. With an email list you add every tester by hand in the Console. With a group, you hand out one address and they join themselves. You need 12 people, so the group is far less work. Two things trip everyone up. First, a new group is locked by default. If you don't change these, testers open your link and only see "content unavailable". - Who can search for the group -> Anyone on the web - Who can join the group -> Anyone can join

Second, linking the group and saving is not enough. If that track has no published build, the opt-in link stays shut and testers see "App not available". They joined the group but can't get the app.

So the order matters. 1. Create the group and fix the two settings above 2. Play Console > Test and release > Closed testing > Testers tab: paste the group address and hit Save 3. Upload an AAB to that track and get it through review 4. Only then invite testers ! Workspace groups (groups.google.com/a/yourdomain/g/...) usually block outside accounts. If you're recruiting testers publicly, make the group on a personal Google account. When you register your app in ACT Party, fill in both the group address and the opt-in link - testers then get the steps in the right order. Full walkthrough with screenshots: https://actparty.app/guides/google-group.html
