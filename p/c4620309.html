You can test either app or both. 1. QuitBloom – Quit Smoking Support An app to help users track their quit-smoking progress and stay motivated. Web Tester Registration: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dreambridgehq.quitbloom Android Installation: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dreambridgehq.quitbloom 2. My First Korean – Beginner Korean Learning An app for people who are starting to learn Korean. Web Tester Registration: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dreambridgehq.firstkorean Android Installation: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dreambridgehq.firstkorean How to Participate: 1. Join our Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/dreambridgehq 2. Use the same Google account to select "Become a tester" for each app. 3. Install your chosen app from its Android link when available. 4. Stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days and try the app. 5. Share any bugs, issues, or suggestions. Important: - Each app requires separate tester registration. - Google Group membership alone does not count as opting in. - Android only. - Installation availability depends on Google Play review and release status. Thank you for supporting independent app development! DreamBridge Development Team
