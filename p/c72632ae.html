⑧ Why testers disappear

zenith.studio · 2026-09-14

Four ways a trade dies. Two of them you can prevent. 1. Your test is not open in their country, so they cannot install ← preventable 2. The group permissions are locked and only you cannot see it ← preventable 3. They install, then quietly uninstall 4. The slow fade Nobody says anything about 1 or 2. You just watch the counter stall. That is why they are both the most common and the hardest to spot. 4 is the biggest. Across 48 finished trades we measured, the average was 8.8 opens out of 14 days. Almost none of it is bad faith — it is people who meant to help and hit friction. https://actparty.app/guides/why-testers-drop.html
