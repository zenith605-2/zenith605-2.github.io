1. Join the Google Group first with the same Gmail as your Play Store, then open the testing link and install. 2. On first launch, pick English (or German) and allow location access. 3. Check if prayer times match your city and wait for one adhan notification. 4. Open the Qibla compass and rotate your phone. 5. Open a few surahs in the Quran reader and try marking prayers as completed. Please keep the app installed for 14 days and open it once in a while. Any feedback is welcome! https://groups.google.com/g/kiblenuma-test https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kiblenuma.kiblenuma
