② Getting testers for YOUR app

zenith.studio · 2026-08-26

This guide is for collecting testers. To test someone else's app, see guide 3. Register the app YOU made in [My Apps] or [Settings > Get testers for my app]. - App name and package - pick it with [Import from installed apps] - Opt-in URL - your tester link from Play Console (play.google.com/apps/testing/...) - Google Group URL - only if you accept testers through a group ! Only your OWN app goes here. This is not where you add apps you test for others. (Adding someone else's app gives a "registered by another account" error.) Turn on [Show on app board] and other developers can find you and offer trades.
