① Turn on Usage Access first

zenith.studio · 2026-08-26

Miss this one thing and nothing else works. The app records how many days and minutes you used each test app - automatically. But reading that requires Android's "Usage Access" permission. Without it, nothing reaches the server. The worst part: there is no visible error. You are testing diligently, but the other developer sees "no record". You look like someone who broke the deal. This has actually happened. Go to Settings > Apps and enable [Usage Access]. If you tapped "Later" on the popup at first launch, you need to turn it on now.

For the record: we only read usage of the apps you registered for testing. We never look at what other apps you have or use.
