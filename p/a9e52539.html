⑩ What Google actually checks

zenith.studio · 2026-09-14

In 12 testers × 14 days, what gets counted is how long they stayed opted in — not how many times they opened your app. Play Console says it plainly: "testers opted in for N days". Someone can join your group, tap Become a tester, install nothing, and still be one of the 12. Uninstalling does not take them off the list either. So why does everyone say testers have to be active? Two reasons, and only one of them is Google. - The 14 days have to be continuous. If someone opts out, or you remove them from the group, their clock goes back to zero. That is what breaks a test, not a quiet Tuesday. - You have to write the form yourself. A test nobody opened leaves you nothing to write, and a reviewer reads what you wrote. A skipped day is not worth worrying about. Someone leaving the group is. https://actparty.app/guides/what-google-checks.html
