③ Testing someone else's app

zenith.studio · 2026-08-26

This guide is for being a tester. There are three ways in. - App board - open [Testing] > [App board] and offer a trade - Already installed - if you installed it from a community post, use [Test someone's app] > [Import from installed apps] - The app asks you - installed something new recently? ACT Party will offer to track it on next launch

After that, just open the app once a day. Check-ins are automatic - no buttons, no screenshots. Under a minute is fine; opens count too. That said, actually using the app for a bit is better for everyone. Google requires 12 testers held for 14 consecutive days. We count consecutive days the same way, so a missed day shows immediately.
