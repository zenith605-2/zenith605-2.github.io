⑪ Nobody opens your app all 14 days

zenith.studio · 2026-09-14

Everyone guesses at this, so we counted. Across 48 trades that ran the full fourteen days, here is how many days the tester actually opened the app. Lowest 0 / 14 Highest 14 / 14 Average 8.8 / 14 Perfect 5 of 48 One person never opened it at all. Two things follow. First, your test is probably fine. A tester who missed three days did not break anything. If everyone had to hit 14 out of 14, nobody would pass. Second, zero out of fourteen is still not testing. Eight days is someone who used your app. Zero is someone who opted in and forgot. Google may not see the difference, but the developer who installed yours in exchange does. https://actparty.app/guides/daily-opens-data.html
