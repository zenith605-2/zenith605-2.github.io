We're adding ads to ACT Party

zenith.studio · 2026-10-04

We're adding ads to ACT Party Hi, I'm the developer of ACT Party. ACT Party has been free and ad-free so far. But as more of you joined, we went past the free limit of our server (Supabase). This month's data transfer hit 12GB, more than twice the 5GB free limit, so we now need a paid plan to keep things running. To cover the server cost, we've decided to add ads. We don't plan to add paid features — everything in ACT Party stays free, and ads will keep it running. We'll start with a banner at the bottom and an ad when you tap [Open all], and adjust based on how it feels to you. One thing we won't do: interrupt the testing flow — joining a group, opting in, installing, or check-ins. The next update also cuts the data the app uses a lot, so it should feel lighter and faster. If you'd rather support us without ads, tips in Settings always help. If anything bothers you, let me know in the comments. Thank you.
