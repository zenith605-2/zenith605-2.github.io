⑦ The five screens that stop a tester

zenith.studio · 2026-09-14

Of 105 "the link doesn't work" reports we got, 87 came in while other people were getting through the same link at the same time. It was not the link. It was that one person's side. The screens all look different, though. Show someone these five and ask which one they saw, and the cause sorts itself out on the spot. 1. Group — "content unavailable" 2. Opt-in — "App not available" 3. Store — "item not found" 4. Console — "more testing needed" 5. Rejection email — two reasons 2 and 3 get mixed up the most, and they are opposites. 2 means the track has no published build. 3 means the opt-in never went through. Screenshots and what each one means: https://actparty.app/guides/blocked-screens.html
