⑨ Open your test to all countries

zenith.studio · 2026-09-14

One app in three loses testers to this. The tester taps your link, gets "not available in your country", and says nothing — they assume they did something wrong. From your side it just looks like one more person who never showed up. Here is the part that catches people: the country list on a test track is separate from production. You can have production open everywhere and your closed test open in four countries, and nothing tells you. Widening countries on a closed test is usually safe. Only people who opted in can see that track at all. Console screenshots and the order to do it in: https://actparty.app/guides/country-availability.html
