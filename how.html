What is ACT Party?

It fills the 12 testers Google requires for a closed test, by having developers test each other's apps. It is an Android app plus this website, and all of it is free.

What it does

App board

Apps looking for testers, one per row. Offer a trade, and it starts when they accept. Browsing needs no account.

Automatic check-ins

The Android app reads usage stats and records which days you opened their app, and for how long. No screenshots, no forms, nobody has to confirm anything.

Check-in calendar

Fourteen days, one cell each. If they use ACT Party too, the days they opened yours show on the row below — so you can see if only one side is showing up.

Install and uninstall detection

Setup completes itself once the app is installed. If someone uninstalls partway through, the other side sees it.

Group and opt-in checks

We check whether your Google Group is visible from outside when you register. And if a tester cannot get in through your opt-in link, one tap tells you — with the exact Play Console screen to fix.

Chat and feedback

One-to-one chat with your trading partner. Testers leave private feedback that nobody else sees, and you can reply to it.

Finished trades and karma

Whether someone stayed the full 14 days, or quit partway, stays on the record and shows on their card. This is what you read before choosing a partner.

Collecting tester emails

If your test takes an email list instead of a group, the addresses collect here and copy out comma-separated in one tap — paste straight into Play Console.

Board

Developers going through the same stage post questions, tips and launch stories. The app and the site show the same threads.

Notifications

Offers, acceptances, messages and feedback arrive as push notifications. Each kind can be switched off.

How a trade runs

  1. Register your app — an opt-in link or package name is enough.
  2. Offer a trade from the board, or accept one that came to your app.
  3. Join their group, opt in, install the app. Setup is done once it is installed.
  4. Open it once a day for 14 days. The record writes itself.
  5. When the period ends and you did not uninstall, it counts as finished.

The app and the site do different jobs

Android app — the daily check-ins and the record. Only the app can read usage stats, so installs are confirmed there too.

This website — registering apps, offering and accepting trades, chatting, replying to feedback. The board is readable without an account.

It does not cost anything

There are no paid features. It is also not a place to buy testers — Google forbids it, and bought testers do not open your app.

What Google requires

Personal developer accounts made after 13 November 2023 need 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before you can publish to production. It counts opt-in time, not how often anyone opened the app.
